LoRA Training Pipeline

Fine-tune Parakeet-TDT using LoRA adapters with labeled KDCA ATC data.

1. Chunk & Label

Use the CLI to chunk recordings and run dual-ASR labeling, then review in the Labeling tab.

atc-recorder training chunk ./recordings atc-recorder training label

2. Normalize & Export

Apply text normalization and export NeMo manifests.

atc-recorder training normalize atc-recorder training manifest

3. Train & Benchmark

Launch NeMo LoRA training and benchmark the result.

docker compose --profile train up atc-recorder training benchmark -t test.json
